## Community Contract Model

GODcoin is **not forcing anyone** to pay rent in GOD. This is a **community contract** — a voluntary agreement between the company and its members.

### How the Community Works

- The company builds housing communities exclusively for GOD holders
- Residents agree to participate in the GOD economy as part of their community membership
- Holding GOD is the entry ticket to the community
- Rent payments through GOD come with **discounts** — rewarding participation
- Non-holders can still rent at market rate, but they don't get community benefits

### Why Strict Rules Matter

> *"If we follow the same rules as everyone else, we'll just be one more token that does nothing."*

GODcoin must be different **by design**. The rules that make it different are:

1. **Community-first**: Buildings are built for GOD holders, not speculators
2. **Real-world backing**: Every expansion is tied to physical property
3. **Mandatory participation**: Community members engage with the GOD economy
4. **No pure speculation**: GOD's value comes from utility, not hype
5. **Giving back**: Profits are reinvested into the community, not extracted
