## Article 1: Community Membership

### 1.1 Who Can Join
Anyone can buy and hold GOD tokens. However, **community membership** — including access to GODcoin housing, discounts, and community services — requires:

- Holding a minimum of **1,000 GOD** tokens
- Agreeing to this Community Contract
- Participating in the GOD economy (using GOD for rent and services)

### 1.2 Membership Tiers

| Tier | Minimum GOD | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | 1,000 GOD | 5% rent discount, community access |
| **Silver** | 10,000 GOD | 10% rent discount, priority housing, community voting |
| **Gold** | 50,000 GOD | 15% rent discount, priority housing, governance participation |
| **Platinum** | 100,000 GOD | 20% rent discount, priority on all new builds, full governance rights |

### 1.3 Membership Verification
- Membership is verified **on-chain** by checking wallet balances
- Members must maintain minimum holdings to keep their tier
- Tier status is checked at the beginning of each month

## Article 2: Rent & Payments

### 2.1 How Rent Works
1. Community members purchase GOD tokens from any exchange (Raydium, Jupiter, etc.)
2. Rent is paid by sending GOD to the designated **Company Wallet**
3. The rent amount in GOD is calculated based on the current market rate at time of payment
4. Members receive their **tier discount** automatically

### 2.2 Company Rights
- The company has full authority to **hold GOD** or **convert GOD to USD** at its discretion
- This decision is based on market conditions and operational needs
- Revenue from rent payments is used to fund community expansion

### 2.3 Payment Schedule
- Rent is due on the **1st of each month**
- Payments must be sent from a **registered community wallet**
- Late payments follow standard community guidelines

### 2.4 Non-GOD Payment Option
- Tenants are **not forced** to pay in GOD
- Market-rate rent in USD is always available
- However, **USD payments do not receive discounts**
- Only GOD payments qualify for community benefits
